A new logo for Stacktape

Stacktape deploys apps and their infrastructure to your own AWS account. On AWS, infrastructure is grouped into "stacks", and the name means "a tape for stacks". So the logo is a piece of tape: in the full logo it has "tape" printed on it, and in the small icon it has a stack.
