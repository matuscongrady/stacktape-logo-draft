A new logo for Stacktape

Stacktape deploys apps and their infrastructure to your own AWS account. On AWS, infrastructure is grouped into "stacks", and the name means "a tape for stacks". So the icon is a stack held together by a strip of tape, and in the full logo "tape" is printed on a piece of tape.
